What we do not collect

  • Try-on photos. A selfie you use in the try-on preview is read by your own browser and is never uploaded to us or stored.
  • Quick-answer questions. The quick-answer helper runs in your browser; what you type is not sent to us.
  • Tracking. This site uses no analytics, advertising or social-media tracking.

Storage in your browser

The site keeps a few small items in your browser so that it works — your bag, your sign-in, a payment in progress. None of them is used for advertising or tracking. The Cookie Policy lists every one.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only where it is needed for the purpose you gave it for:

  • Payment provider. When online payment is available and you choose it, payment is taken by Razorpay, which receives the details you enter on its form. If you pay by bank transfer or UPI yourself, your bank and ours handle that payment.
  • Email delivery. Order and account emails are sent through an email delivery service: To be provided: name of the email delivery provider.
  • Hosting. The website and its database run on To be provided: hosting provider and where the data is stored.
  • Delivery. Your delivery name, phone number and address go to the courier who delivers your order: To be provided: courier or delivery partners used.
  • Fonts. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • When the law requires it. We disclose data to authorities where we are legally required to.

We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data for as long as the purpose it was collected for needs it, and then delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it longer.

  • Accounts and saved addresses: To be provided: retention period for inactive accounts.

Your rights and choices

  • See and correct your details. Signed in, you can view and update your profile and saved addresses yourself.
  • Stop marketing emails. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Withdrawing is as easy as signing up was, and it does not affect your orders or account.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or erasure of your personal data, or nominate someone to act for you, by emailing from the email address on your account (or giving your order number).
  • Complain. Tell us first at the same address. We will respond within To be provided: response period for privacy requests and grievances — no more than 90 days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Account deletion is not yet available as a button on the site; until it is, it is done on request.

Children

This shop is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please do not create an account or place an order yourself; a parent or guardian can order for you.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, never in a form anyone can read. The site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to order and customer data is limited to signed-in shop staff.

If a breach of personal data occurs, we will tell the people affected and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

The law this follows

This policy is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (notified 13 November 2025). Most of their obligations come into force eighteen months after notification.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and its date.
