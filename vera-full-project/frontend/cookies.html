This page sets out what this website stores in your browser, and why.

In short

This site does not set cookies, and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. It keeps a few small items in your browser's own storage so that the shop works. Because every one of them is needed for something you asked the site to do, there is no cookie banner to accept or decline.

Everything the site stores

Items stored in your browser
NameWhat it holdsWhyHow long
hairshalo.cart.v1The products, options and quantities in your bagKeeps your bag between visitsUntil you empty the bag or clear your browser
vera.customerTokenA sign-in tokenKeeps you signed inUntil you sign out; the token itself expires
vera.pendingPaymentThe order you are paying forLets you finish a payment if the tab closes midwayUntil the payment completes, or you clear your browser
vera.currencyThe currency you chose to view prices inRemembers your choiceUntil you change it or clear your browser
vera.introThat the opening animation has playedSo it does not replay on every page loadUntil you close the tab

Other services

  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces; Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Razorpay is loaded only when you choose to pay online, and may use its own cookies for that payment under its own policy.

Your control

You can clear these items at any time in your browser's settings. The shop will still work; you will be signed out and your bag will be empty.

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential technology, it will not run until you have chosen to allow it, and you will be able to change that choice from every page.
