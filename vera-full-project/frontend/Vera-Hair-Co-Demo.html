Questions, answered

How do I choose the right cap size?

We send a free sizing kit with a measuring guide, or you can book a virtual fitting and our stylists will measure with you over video call.

How long does a human hair wig last?

With proper washing, conditioning, and storage, most customers get 12–18 months of regular wear from a single unit.

Can I colour or heat style the hair?

Yes — since it's 100% human hair, it can be safely heat-styled up to 180°C and coloured up to two shades darker at a professional salon.

Do you offer returns or exchanges?

Unworn units in original packaging can be exchanged within 7 days. Our fit guarantee covers sizing issues on your first order.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders ship within 24 hours and arrive in 3–5 business days across India, with live tracking sent to your email and WhatsApp.
